    What to do now

    1. Stop. Don't pay and don't share any numbers or codes.
    2. Hang up or don't reply. Don't use the number, link, or button they gave you.
    3. Look it up yourself. Find the real number on your card, your statement, or the official website, and call that.
    4. Talk to someone you trust before you do anything.

    Already paid? Call whoever handled the money right away (your bank, the gift card company, the wire service, or the payment app), say it was fraud, and ask them to stop or reverse it. Then report it.
